Five clickable phone-frame prototypes. Everything tappable is wired: grids open shows, episodes open players, mini-players expand, tabs switch. Chips under each phone jump straight to a screen. All content is synthetic.

A
Shelf
Pocket Casts lineage · light · artwork-forward
Bottom nav with five tabs, 3-column artwork grid with unplayed badges, floating rounded mini-player, full player with Notes / Transcript / Chapters tabs. The "safe default" — familiar to any podcast-app user.
bottom navartwork gridfloating mini-player
B
Cockpit
Smart AudioBook Player lineage · dark · giant controls
Player is the home screen. Oversized buttons you can hit without looking, chapter + book progress bars, tile shortcuts for speed/sleep/bookmark. Library is one tap away, with big rows and time-remaining.
player-firsthuge targetsglance-free use
C
Flow
Queue-first · dark gradient · mixed-media
Home is your Up Next queue — podcast episodes and audiobook chapters interleaved with type badges, a now-playing hero card on top. Built around Orator's mixed playlists (Phase 5). Modern glass-card look.
queue homemixed mediahero card
D
Ledger
Text-forward · paper light · maximum density
No bottom nav — slim top tabs, dense monospace-accented rows, tiny glyphs for state (downloaded, in-progress, transcript). Compact two-line docked player. For power users who want information, not chrome.
dense liststop tabsdocked player
E
Boost
Overcast lineage · light · playlist-centric
iOS-style sectioned home: pinned playlists up top (Queue, All New, Favorites, Audiobooks), then podcasts as a list with latest-episode previews. Tinted player with paged dots (art / notes / chapters), chapter ticks on the scrubber, and a bottom Effects sheet — Smart Speed with "time saved", Voice Boost, per-show override toggle.
